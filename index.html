Flagship · Sensor data → ML-ready datasets

The training-data pipeline resp → dogapi

I sole-architected resp (984 commits), the experimental pipeline where the hard parts were invented and stress-tested — GOR sniff detection, LSL↔Unix clock synchronization, three-way sample attribution, a logical-session resolver, and a versioned star-schema analytics layer — then hardened the proven logic into dogapi, the production FastAPI backend where I'm the top contributor and sole author of the dataset-generation and session-validation vertical. Together they fuse five independent evidence streams (IR array, wireless LSL, vision, QR, trainer-app state) into one confidence-tiered record per sniff, behind a physiological validation gate that keeps only trustworthy sessions in the training set.

Neuro · signal processing & ML

Canine EEG studies

An end-to-end EEG pipeline and studies on a g.tec rig. In an auditory-oddball ERP study I traced a ~1.45 s marker-timing bug that had nulled the effect, then proved the recovered response was neural — not cardiac — via R-peak subtraction and permutation testing. I also built a from-scratch ECG-on-EEG channel-viability detector, validated with nested leave-one-recording-out CV.

MNE-Python ERP / oddball Permutation testing LORO cross-validation

Diagnostics · data integrity

Data-integrity forensics

Root-caused silent data-corruption bugs in the training pipeline: crash-restart session contamination, a session that captured only ~57% of its duration, and a structural ~30% sample-code mismatch traced to a QR-scan vs TimescaleDB timing race — each with per-timestamp evidence and a precise query-window fix.
