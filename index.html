Flagship · Design-history & quality engine

otvcore

A system that turns plain-language descriptions of engineering decisions into controlled, auditable records — Operational Test & Verification results, Non-Conformity Reports, engineering change orders and SOPs — over a git-backed, tamper-evident store where every mutation is a single verifiable commit and a failed write rolls the whole change back. Architected as one engine, three clients: a FastAPI service with a React/TypeScript UI, a command-line tool, and an agent skill, kept from diverging by a shared core. Built test-first through a human-gated, agent-assisted workflow — evidence before claims, changes behind review — with the suite growing from 16 tests to 483 over twenty-six days.

Olfaction platform

SniffSpace

A scent-detection platform where trained dogs evaluate samples while their physiology is recorded. I contribute to the multi-sensor acquisition pipeline — EEG, respiration, IMU and video — with Lab Streaming Layer synchronization, session logging, and data-quality verification across recording runs.

Canine brain–computer interface

DogSense

A wearable EEG system for dogs: a 3D-printed TPU helmet with a defined electrode montage, spring-loaded holders and an integrated amplifier. I work on signal acquisition and clock synchronization, and on machine-learning models that classify neural responses from the recorded EEG.
